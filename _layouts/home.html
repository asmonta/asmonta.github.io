Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of contributing to cutting-edge projects at LANL, NAVIAR, ARC, and VIPR, where I’ve honed my expertise in Engineering Design, Optimization, Simulation, and Additive Manufacturing. My work bridges the gap between academic research and practical applications, driving innovation in every project I undertake.

Beyond my technical work, I’m passionate about mentoring the next generation of engineers. I’ve advised undergraduate and graduate students, led workshops, and shared my knowledge to help others grow. As I progress in my career, I aim to transition into leadership roles, leveraging my technical expertise to guide teams and foster innovation.

This website is a showcase of my independent projects. Feel free to explore the tabs to learn more about my work. Thank you for visiting, and I hope you find something inspiring here!
